SOD Marks Filler

Reads the students' marksheet PDFs, works out Max Marks, Max Credits, Obtained Marks, Obtained Credit Points and SGPA for every semester plus the Overall block with CGPA, and puts them into the SOD tracking sheet in front of the right student. Only empty marks cells are filled — nothing else in the file is changed, and everything is re-checked before you can download it.

How the numbers are made

  1. Max Marks = number of courses in the semester × 100 · Max Credits = Σ (T + P + PR) — must equal the "CREDITS:" printed on the marksheet.
  2. Obtained Marks = Σ marks · Obtained Credit Points = Σ (course credits × grade points of the printed grade: O 10, A+ 9, A 8, B+ 7, B 6, C 5, P 4, F 0).
  3. Overall = sum of the semesters; it must agree with MAX. MARKS / OBTAINED MARKS / TOTAL CREDITS printed on the marksheet. CGPA and SGPA: see the choice below.
  4. The student is found by Registration No. = "Registration Id", and name, programme and specialization are cross-checked.
  5. Semesters must match the category: Regular — B.Des 1–8, M.Voc 1–4, B.Voc 1–6; Lateral Entry (2nd Year) — B.Des 3–8, M.Voc 3–4, B.Voc 3–6; Lateral Entry (3rd Year) — B.Des 5–8, B.Voc 5–6.
  6. A student is not written at all if anything does not add up (and is listed with the reason). A cell that already has a different value is never overwritten.
